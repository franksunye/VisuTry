# YouTube Community Map

Last reviewed: 2026-08-24
Review rule: prioritize active comment sections and actual shopper questions; YouTube is not a backlink channel.

## Topic map

| Topic | Intent | What to look for | Interaction mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| How to choose glasses | High | Questions about shape, proportions, and confidence | Reply with a practical decision framework |
| Glasses for face shape | High | Shape rules being over-applied or misunderstood | Add nuance: width, eye position, lens height, style |
| Glasses sizing and fit | High | Frame width, bridge, temple, lens height | Explain what to check before buying |
| Eyewear and brand reviews | Purchase/comparison | Users choosing between frames or brands | Answer the concrete comparison question |
| Virtual try-on and makeovers | Tool evaluation | Trust, scale, realism, and limitations | Explain what image-based tools can and cannot establish |
| Styling tips | Medium | Color contrast, geometry, visual weight | Give a specific, standalone style observation |

## Seed query pool

`how to choose glasses`, `glasses for face shape`, `glasses styling`, `best glasses for round face`, `best glasses for oval face`, `buying glasses online`, `glasses sizing`, `frame width`, `eyewear review`, `Zenni review`, `Warby Parker review`, `virtual try on glasses`, `glasses makeover`, `which glasses suit me`, `eyewear styling tips`.

## Qualification checklist

- Video and comments are relevant to eyewear decision-making.
- Comments are recent enough to support a real conversation.
- A user has asked a concrete question or expressed uncertainty.
- The response can stand alone without a VisuTry mention.
- The response is not a generic compliment or a mechanical link drop.
