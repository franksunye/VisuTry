# VisuTry Product Advantage Gate

**Status:** Active pre-outreach execution gate  
**Created:** 2026-08-24  
**Owner:** Product / Engineering / Growth  
**Purpose:** Define the three product advantages VisuTry must prove before structured merchant outreach becomes the primary company motion.

---

## 1. Why this gate exists

VisuTry has already completed the core 2B product architecture, Reference Factory, Discover/distribution baseline, Business Website v1.2, durable Pilot intake, Store / Campaign runtime, Commerce Intelligence, Merchant Workspace, and the first production Agent-native access path.

The next phase is **not yet broad merchant outreach**.

Before structured outbound begins, VisuTry must raise three strategic capabilities to a clearly defensible market bar:

1. **Consumer Distribution & Proof**
2. **Merchant Experience Excellence**
3. **Agent-Native Merchant Operations**

These are not independent feature tracks. Together they determine how difficult VisuTry is to sell, how differentiated the product is, and how efficiently a merchant can adopt and operate it.

Operating principle:

> **Do not use outbound sales to compensate for product, distribution, or onboarding weakness. Build the three leverage layers to the required bar first, then begin structured outreach from a stronger position.**

The goal of this phase is not feature completeness. The goal is to establish three measurable advantages:

> **VisuTry can attract high-intent eyewear shoppers.**  
> **VisuTry can deliver a brand-grade commerce experience.**  
> **VisuTry can be operated by an AI agent with near-zero merchant onboarding effort.**

Only after all three gates are passed should controlled merchant outreach become the primary motion.

---

## 2. Pillar A — Consumer Distribution & Proof Engine

The Consumer product is not only a standalone B2C experience.

It is also VisuTry's:

- acquisition layer;
- product-quality proof;
- eyewear intent data source;
- SEO / AEO / GEO surface;
- AI-agent discovery surface;
- future natural-distribution layer for Merchant Stores and Campaigns.

The strategic model is:

```text
Search / Content / Social / AI Assistants
                ↓
        VisuTry Consumer Experience
                ↓
Face Understanding → Recommendation
                ↓
       Try-On → Compare
                ↓
     Measurable Shopper Intent
                ↓
 Merchant Store / Campaign / Product
```

Consumer growth therefore creates value twice:

1. it grows VisuTry's own consumer traffic and data;
2. it increases the value VisuTry can eventually deliver to merchants.

The long-term merchant proposition should not be limited to:

> “Use VisuTry to convert the traffic you already buy.”

It should increasingly become:

> **“Use VisuTry to convert your paid traffic and participate in an eyewear discovery network that also attracts organic search and AI-agent traffic.”**

A normal campaign platform primarily consumes merchant-acquired traffic. VisuTry should progressively become capable of both:

> **Traffic Capture + Traffic Conversion**

### 2.1 Required Consumer Experience bar

Before this pillar is considered ready:

- Face Analysis, Glasses Advisor, Try-On and Compare must feel like one coherent eyewear decision journey.
- Mobile and desktop must both meet a premium consumer-product quality bar.
- The primary journey must not contain obvious visual, interaction, latency or continuity debt.
- The strongest VisuTry capabilities must be easy to discover rather than buried in isolated tools.
- The experience should be competitive with strong consumer eyewear products, not merely technically functional.
- The strongest vertical-specific experience should aim to exceed generic VTO flows by combining face understanding, advice, try-on and comparison into one decision journey.

### 2.2 Required SEO / AEO / GEO bar

- Important eyewear-decision intents must have clear, indexable and answerable surfaces.
- Content and product pages must be structured so search engines and AI assistants can understand the question, answer, entity and next action.
- VisuTry must be able to distinguish organic search traffic from AI-assistant / agent traffic.
- AI referral behavior must be measured rather than discussed only as a future opportunity.
- Search / AI discovery must connect naturally into useful product journeys rather than terminate on informational pages.
- High-value visual SEO assets must be mapped to relevant search-intent pages and work on both desktop and mobile.
- Evidence should include Search Console / analytics coverage, attributable AI-referral traffic, and query-to-product continuation where technically observable.

### 2.3 Proof requirement

The gate is passed only when VisuTry can produce evidence for:

```text
Discovery
→ Visit
→ Useful decision interaction
→ Recommendation / Try-On / Compare
→ Intent
```

and separately observe or classify:

```text
Search traffic
AI-assistant / Agent traffic
Direct / Social / Referral traffic
```

The objective is not a vanity SEO score.

The objective is to demonstrate:

> **VisuTry can acquire qualified eyewear intent without buying every visit.**

### 2.4 Pillar A Definition of Done

Pillar A is complete when:

1. the core Consumer decision journey has passed a structured desktop + mobile experience audit with no unresolved P0/P1 issues;
2. priority eyewear intents have production SEO/AEO/GEO surfaces with correct indexability, metadata, structured data where appropriate, internal linking and useful product continuation;
3. AI-assistant / agent referrals can be identified or classified with a documented measurement method;
4. VisuTry can show evidence of organic and/or AI-origin sessions reaching meaningful product interaction;
5. the resulting evidence can be reused in the 2B sales narrative without unsupported claims.

## 3. Pillar B — Merchant Experience Excellence

Store and Campaign are commercially familiar categories. Merchants and agencies have had landing pages, campaign microsites, ecommerce experiences, VTO tools and marketing platforms for years.

Therefore:

> **“It works” is not an acceptable competitive bar.**

A merchant is not buying the existence of a mini-site. A merchant is buying a digital experience that represents its brand in front of paid and organic traffic.

A Campaign may receive traffic generated by:

- Meta;
- Google;
- TikTok;
- email;
- creators;
- QR;
- affiliates;
- AI assistants;
- merchant-owned channels.

If a brand is willing to send meaningful traffic into VisuTry, the destination must be professionally credible.

The product model remains:

```text
Traffic
  ↓
Store / Campaign Experience
  ↓
Shopper Understanding
  ↓
Personalized Recommendation
  ↓
Try-On
  ↓
Compare
  ↓
Product / Inquiry Intent
  ↓
Merchant Commerce System
```

### 3.1 Store / Campaign shopper surface bar

Before this pillar is considered ready:

- Store and Campaign shopper surfaces must feel brand-grade rather than startup-demo-grade.
- Brand identity, typography, spacing, imagery and product presentation must be credible for premium eyewear.
- Campaign-specific narrative, product subset and context must visibly affect the experience; Campaign cannot feel like a renamed Store.
- Recommendation → Try-On → Compare → CTA must feel like a single conversion experience.
- Desktop and mobile layouts must both be production quality.
- Loading, empty, error, processing and completed states must be intentionally designed.
- Performance must be acceptable for campaign traffic and should not be dominated by avoidable heavy assets or unnecessary model/network cost.
- Marketing screenshots and Business Website assets must reflect the current real Store / Campaign / Admin product rather than obsolete UI.

### 3.2 Merchant / Admin bar

- Store, Campaign, Catalog and Commerce Intelligence hierarchy must be immediately understandable.
- Important actions must look like merchant operations, not engineering controls.
- Merchant-facing copy must be clear enough for a brand or agency operator without founder explanation.
- Analytics should emphasize business signals such as Visitors, Engaged Shoppers, Recommendation Rate, Try-On Rate, Compare Rate, Product Click, Inquiry / Lead, High-Intent Shoppers and Acquisition Source when those signals are legitimately available.
- Reports must not imply unsupported revenue, ROAS or sales-lift attribution.
- The Admin / Merchant experience should be visually credible enough to appear in a client review or campaign performance meeting.

### 3.3 Business acquisition surface bar

The `/business` website must visually and factually match the product being sold.

A brand, ecommerce lead, or agency account lead should be able to move through:

```text
Business Website
→ Example
→ Store / Campaign
→ Shopper Experience
→ Merchant Insight
→ Pilot
```

without the product quality dropping materially between surfaces.

The primary review personas are:

- premium eyewear brand marketing lead;
- ecommerce director;
- performance marketing lead;
- digital / 4A agency account or strategy lead.

### 3.4 Pillar B Definition of Done

Pillar B is complete when:

1. Business Website, Store, Campaign, focused Try-On workspace and Merchant/Admin surfaces have passed a second structured visual/workflow review on desktop and mobile;
2. all screenshots / marketing product visuals used by `/business` match current production concepts and interfaces;
3. no unresolved P0/P1 visual or primary-flow issue remains in the main demonstration and purchase journeys;
4. at least the current Reference portfolio can demonstrate materially differentiated Store and Campaign experiences without merchant-specific runtime forks;
5. the complete experience is credible enough that VisuTry would confidently direct a brand's paid campaign traffic into it.

## 4. Pillar C — Agent-Native Merchant Operations

Agent-native operation is not merely an MCP integration feature. It is a core scalability mechanism.

The business objective is:

> **Merchant onboarding effort → as close to zero as reasonably possible.**

Traditional merchant software often requires:

```text
Sales
→ onboarding meeting
→ catalog preparation
→ configuration
→ implementation
→ QA
→ launch
→ reporting
→ optimization
```

VisuTry should progressively compress this into:

```text
Merchant authorization
        ↓
AI Agent
        ↓
Catalog intake
        ↓
Store / Campaign creation
        ↓
Configuration
        ↓
Preview
        ↓
Merchant approval
        ↓
Publish
        ↓
Analytics / optimization
```

VisuTry already has the architectural foundation: Merchant Workspace, Agent Keys, Remote MCP, OAuth, Store / Campaign tools, aggregate Commerce Intelligence and a production Codex Golden Path.

The remaining question is no longer whether the architecture is possible. It is whether the complete commercial workflow can be autonomously operated.

### 4.1 Agent-Native Golden Path

A supported agent such as Codex or Cursor should be able to receive a business-level instruction such as:

> “Create a Summer Sunglasses campaign from my catalog for women aged 25–34 coming from Meta. Prepare a preview and wait for my approval before publishing.”

Without developer intervention, the agent should be able to:

1. authenticate to the correct Merchant Workspace;
2. inspect merchant and catalog state;
3. ingest a supported catalog source or use an existing catalog;
4. validate catalog readiness and explain validation failures;
5. select the relevant catalog subset;
6. create or configure the Store / Campaign;
7. configure supported presentation and campaign context;
8. generate or retrieve a preview;
9. summarize exactly what will be published;
10. request explicit merchant approval;
11. publish only after approval;
12. retrieve Commerce Intelligence after traffic arrives;
13. explain results and recommend supported next actions.

### 4.2 Required safety and operating boundary

The system must preserve:

- merchant tenancy;
- scoped authorization;
- explicit approval for consequential publish operations;
- auditability;
- idempotency;
- safe preview-before-publish behavior;
- no raw secret exposure;
- no direct database intervention as part of the normal Golden Path;
- bounded and documented error recovery.

### 4.3 Pillar C Definition of Done

The gate is not passed merely because tools exist.

It is passed when:

> **Codex and Cursor can independently complete the defined merchant Golden Path using documented VisuTry capabilities, without hidden developer knowledge or direct database intervention.**

Pillar C is complete when:

1. Codex passes the full current Golden Path from authenticated workspace inspection through catalog, Store/Campaign preview, explicit approval, publish and analytics;
2. Cursor passes the same bounded Golden Path, or any incompatibility is resolved at the standards-based VisuTry boundary rather than by creating a client-specific backend;
3. OAuth authorization lifecycle visibility and revoke behavior required for external merchant use are available;
4. expired authorization artifacts are safely cleaned up;
5. the full path is documented as an operator/agent skill and can be repeated against an isolated real database merchant fixture;
6. normal execution does not require a VisuTry developer to manually prepare database rows, credentials, campaign state or publication state.

## 5. The Three-Pillar Flywheel

These three capabilities reinforce each other:

```text
Excellent Consumer Experience
        ↓
SEO / GEO / AI-agent discovery
        ↓
More high-intent eyewear traffic
        ↓
Better shopper-intent data
        ↓
Stronger Merchant value proposition
        ↓
More Store / Campaign supply
        ↓
More products, campaigns and structured commerce objects
        ↓
Better discovery surface for Search + Agents
```

Agent Native accelerates the same system:

```text
Agent-Native Operations
        ↓
Lower onboarding effort
        ↓
Lower servicing cost
        ↓
More merchants / campaigns can be supported
        ↓
More catalog and campaign supply
        ↓
More distribution and commerce intelligence
```

The strategic result should be:

> **Distribution × Experience × Automation**

rather than:

> Features × Sales effort

## 6. Outreach Readiness Gate

Structured merchant outreach becomes the primary motion only after all three pillars pass their bar.

| Gate | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **A — Consumer Distribution & Proof** | Consumer decision journey meets the target experience bar; SEO/AEO/GEO surfaces are implemented and measurable; organic and AI-agent traffic can be separately observed/classified and connected to meaningful product engagement. |
| **B — Merchant Experience Excellence** | Business → Store/Campaign → shopper journey → Merchant Insight forms one professionally credible brand/agency-grade experience on desktop and mobile. |
| **C — Agent-Native Operations** | Codex and Cursor can complete the agreed merchant Golden Path from workspace/catalog inspection through preview, approval, publish and analytics without developer intervention. |

**Controlled Founding Merchant Outreach is GATED until A + B + C are complete.**

Passing the gate does not require a perfect or finished platform. It requires enough product advantage that outbound sales amplifies strength rather than compensates for weakness.

After the gate passes, the primary execution sequence becomes:

```text
Founding Merchant Outreach
→ First serious Pilot conversations
→ Paid / deposit-backed Pilot
→ Real Catalog
→ Real Traffic
→ Merchant Evidence Loop
→ Evidence-driven product expansion
```

## 8. Explicit non-goals before the gate passes

Do not start or expand the following unless required to pass one of the three gates:

- Reference Brand 6 merely for portfolio size;
- generalized visual Campaign Builder;
- Shopify public app;
- CRM / marketing automation integration;
- revenue attribution / ROAS claims;
- merchant-specific runtime forks;
- second recommendation or Try-On engine;
- broad new enterprise permissions architecture;
- client-specific MCP backends;
- unrelated 2B feature breadth.

The rule is:

> **Every pre-outreach engineering task must map directly to Gate A, Gate B, Gate C, or production stability.**

---

## 9. Exit review

The Product Advantage Gate should be closed by an explicit evidence review, not by elapsed time.

The review must answer:

1. **Distribution:** Can VisuTry prove that search and/or AI discovery reaches meaningful eyewear decision interactions?
2. **Experience:** Would we confidently send a premium brand's paid traffic into the current Store / Campaign experience?
3. **Automation:** Can Codex and Cursor operate the merchant Golden Path without developer intervention?
4. **Claims:** Can the resulting evidence be used in sales without implying unsupported revenue or customer outcomes?
5. **Stability:** Did the work preserve the production Consumer and Merchant critical paths?

If all five answers are YES, move Controlled Founding Merchant Outreach from gated to active.

If any answer is NO, continue only the bounded work required to close that specific gap.
