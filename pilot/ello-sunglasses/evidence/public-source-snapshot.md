## Verified facts used

The size guide describes six petite-fit styles and publishes frame, lens, bridge and temple measurements. It describes Bali as aviator/metal, Bar Harbor as round/acetate, Half Moon Bay as cat-eye/acetate, Outer Banks as lightweight TR90, Pebble Beach as rectangular/acetate, and South Beach as lightweight TR90 with a distinctive shape. The product JSON endpoints exposed the selected variant names, prices, availability and variant-specific images on the snapshot date.

The homepage and size guide describe ello as focused on petite faces/small heads, and state that the lenses are polarized, scratch-resistant and UV400. The pilot stores these statements as source notes only; the shopper recommendation copy does not turn them into a guaranteed-fit or medical claim.

## Fact boundary

- Merchant/public source facts: name, public product destinations, image URLs, variant labels, price/currency, availability snapshot, dimensions, materials and lens claims.
- Operator enrichment: normalized shape for ambiguous silhouettes, relative `width_class`, style tags and collection tags. These are reviewable in `enrichment-review.csv` and must not be presented as merchant-certified measurements.
- Not included: inventory quantity, shipping guarantee, legal endorsement, customer conversion, revenue, or real brand partnership status.
